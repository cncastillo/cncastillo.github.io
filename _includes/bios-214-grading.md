## Assessment and grading

Grading uses a point system, with points earned through attendance and completed laboratory activities.

| Activity | Points each | Number | Total |
|:---------|------------:|-------:|------:|
| Lecture attendance (33.<span class="recurring">3</span>%) | 2 | 9 | 18 |
| Completed regular laboratory (33.<span class="recurring">3</span>%) | 3 | 6 | 18 |
| Completed scanner laboratory (33.<span class="recurring">3</span>%) | 6 | 3 | 18 |
| **Total** | | | **54** |
{: .grading}

<div class="pass-requirement" markdown="1">

**Required to pass: 45 points.**

</div>

- **Lecture attendance:** Attend and record attendance using the Canvas check-in form.
- **Coding laboratory:** Use the skill; submit a reproducible notebook with expected results and prompt history.
- **Scanner laboratory:** Prepare and scan a Pulseq sequence; compare measurements with simulations.

**Submission deadline:** Submit laboratory work through Canvas by **midnight at the end of the day**.

**Additional points:** Giving back to the projects we use is a central part of open source. Contributions earn additional points that count toward the passing requirement:

- **1 point** for an issue, bug, or enhancement report with a minimal working example (MWE).
- **3 points** for a pull request approved through the project's review process.

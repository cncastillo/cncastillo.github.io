## AI policy

**AI use is encouraged in this course.** You may use AI to generate all code, debug implementations, produce visualizations, and explore concepts. The goal is to reduce programming barriers so you can focus on MRI physics, experimental design, and interpretation.

AI can help you find information and write code, but **it does not replace understanding**. **We are here to learn!** You must understand what your submitted code does and how it relates to the physics being studied. You should be able to explain your assumptions, justify your design choices, predict outcomes, and validate results.

**You are responsible for any errors in your submitted work**, including those introduced by AI tools. Assessment will emphasize scientific understanding and your ability to explain and evaluate your work, and may include individual questions or discussions without AI.
